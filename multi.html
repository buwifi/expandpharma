About us

More than just a consultant, ExpandPharma will be an extension of your company in Turkey by working as a part of your team and acting on your behalf.

ExpandPharma provides an integrated suite of services and solutions for pharmaceutical companies from strategy to execution.

With a growing population of 85 million, the implementation of universal healthcare coverage, it's competitive location and strong export network, Turkey is one of the world's most promising pharmaceutical markets.

Established in 2014 in İstanbul, ExpandPharma Consulting provides a portfolio of tailor-made solutions to pharmaceutical companies to realize the full potential of their products in Turkey.

  • Find many collaboration and partnership opportunities
  • Get your products on the market much faster
