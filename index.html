Business Development

We offer help for Turkey Market Entry Solutions

The pharmaceutical market in Turkey is one of the largest and fastest growing markets in the Middle East and North Africa region. With a population of more than 80 million, Turkey offers a large and diverse market for pharmaceutical products.

Global pharmaceutical companies are headquartered in Turkey with significant manufacturing operations using Turkey as a hub for access to regional markets. Successful partnerships are formed with local companies to grow in the region, leveraging the experience and networks of local people.

Turkey is a candidate to be one of the global pharma leaders with its competitive position and export network.

We offer help for the following Market Entry Solutions

Foreign subsidiary establishment

Establishment of the local legal entity in Turkey, which is required to have marketing authorization for pharmaceutical products.

  • Setting up an office/legal entity with a min. cost
  • Market research, competitive analysis and initial sales forecasting
  • Regulatory Affairs and Market Access management
  • Establishing pharmacovigilance system
  • Assessment of vendors when needed (like 3PL, translation agent, customs agent, financial advisor, contract pharmacovigilance service provider etc.)

Distributorship agreements

Distributorship agreements with local companies for licensing, sale and distribution of the products.

  • Search for potential partners in Turkey for the chosen products
  • Arrangement of meetings with potential partners
  • Assisting in contract negotiations
  • Providing guidance during the implementation phase

Strategic Alliance

Co-development, contract manufacturing, out-licensing and in-licensing deals with Turkish companies.

  • Getting in contact with the potential partners
  • Arranging and conducting on-site visits
  • Assisting in contract negotiations
  • Providing guidance during the implementation phase

Market Access

Giving strategic advice for all the market access options available in Turkey

Market access includes generating and communicating data for stakeholders involved in drug adoption and financing, and ensuring that products are priced and reimbursed fairly.

Assessing the burden of disease, sizing the market, developing appropriate pricing, measuring the regulatory and policy environment and implementing the most appropriate distribution approach are the major subjects.

Market access options available in Turkey:

  • Applying for marketing authorisation
  • Named patient programme
  • Compassionate use programme

Our services for Market Access Options

Market Assessment

Initial market assessment helps with undestanding market and competition.

  • Market landscape analysis
  • Competitor analysis
  • Pricing and Positioning Analysis
  • Key Opinion Leader Surveys

Pricing

Points to be considered in pricing in Turkey are government's pricing policies and restrictions, currency fluctuations, customers' solvency, the nature of competition and market entry mode.

  • Determining the optimum price level for the products according to local regulations and competition in the market

Reimbursement

In Turkey, most of the prescribed drugs are included in the Social Security Institution (“SSI”) reimbursement list, and the payments for the treatments are fully or partially paid by the SSI. The national health insurance covers more than 98% of the overall population.

  • Developing scenarios to discover the right access path at country level.
  • Preparation of a Turkey specific pharmacoeconomic evaluation and application dossier for reimbursement approval.

Regulatory Affairs

Ensuring regulatory compliance and optimizing the time to obtain approval of the products in Turkey

We provide following services for Marketing Authorization Applications:

  • Preparation of registration dossiers in eCTD format and according to country specific requirements
  • Preparation and revision of national SPC, PIL and labelling
  • Preparation of application dossiers for Marketing Authorization Transfer
  • Giving advice and assistance for compliance with the local requirements
